- Other miscellaneous happenings and ramblings

## Why not just post on Facebook/Instagram/Twitter?

This is a fantastic question, which in fact I may write an entire blog post on in the future. For the sake of brevity, I will leave it at this: I feel that large, profit-driven social media platforms have been a bane to our society in many ways. They are the quintessential example of [enshittification](https://pluralistic.net/2023/01/21/potemkin-ai/#hey-guys). 

Yet, there are other ways for us to use technology to connect that don't depend on corporations which are driven to extract value from their users. I can't change the state of big social media's influence in our society, but I can share my own content independently of those platforms, so that is what I am doing. I purchased a domain name and pay a hosting provider to host the content I create for this blog. This all costs in the ballpark of $30 per year. I feel that this is a relatively small price to pay to be able to totally own all of the content I am posting, and to share it completely free of ads and algorithms.

Bluesky is a new social media network built on [atproto](https://atproto.com/guides/understanding-atproto). In layman's terms, this means that the content you read and post to Bluesky is in a standard, open format. There is nothing preventing you from developing your own software which interoperates with Bluesky and running it on your own server. This creates a truly competitive environment, where if Bluesky were to enshittify, users would be able to migrate to [another platform](https://frankhecker.com/2026/05/16/from-bluesky-to-blacksky/) with minimal disruption. I created a [Bluesky account](https://bsky.app/profile/bpieper7.bsky.social) a few months ago and have been lurking for a while. I intend to cross-post updates to my blog there.
