Hello, I'm Ben!

Growing up I was fascinated by computers and video games. I had a deep desire to understand how they worked. A computer from my father's work, lots of free time, and internet access were all I needed to teach myself how to write computer programs in middle school. My passion for learning and making things continued into university, where I worked on the electrical systems of solar vehicles for [Team PrISUm](https://www.prisum.org/) at Iowa State. This eventually led to my professional career in DRAM test at Micron Technology.

Another pillar of my childhood was my involvement in Boy Scouts. My curiosity was not confined to computers - the exploration and adventure of nature drew me in as well. In college I started rock climbing and fell in love with the movement, adventure, and community of the sport. I graduated and moved west to Boise, Idaho. I still feel like a kid in a candy store with the sheer amount of mountains, crags, and open spaces accessible to me in the Pacific Northwest.
